What you can learn

  • Japanese dictionary: 20,791 words, explained in 13 languages. Every JLPT N5–N1 word, plus words people often look up: anime and manga words, slang, onomatopoeia, proverbs, business Japanese, place names and Japanese names. Each word has its reading, romaji, audio, example sentences with furigana and a short quiz.
  • English dictionary: 25,955 words with meanings in 12 languages, pronunciation, example sentences and synonyms.
  • Courses: JLPT N5 to N1 vocabulary and reading in Vietnamese, Indonesian, Chinese and Korean. IELTS and TOEFL vocabulary, synonyms by score level and academic reading in Vietnamese, Chinese and Korean.
  • Maths worksheets: for 22 school curricula in 20 countries and regions, from kindergarten to the last year of school, each in the language of the country: United States, Texas (USA), United Kingdom, Canada · Ontario, Canada · British Columbia, Australia, Singapore, India, South Africa, Philippines, Germany, Switzerland, France, Italy, Spain, Mexico, Brazil, South Korea, China, Taiwan, Indonesia, Türkiye. Each topic has a short lesson, 100 practice questions and a printable worksheet with answers.
  • Games: Tangoda, a typing game for hiragana, katakana and basic Japanese words, with audio and meanings in 9 languages.

Made for you

  • Your language. Explanations are written in the language you read best.
  • Your goal. Every page matches a real exam level or a real school year, so you only study what you need.
  • Your pace. About 10 minutes a day on your phone. Questions you miss come back until you know them.
  • Free. No sign-up, no fees. Your progress is saved in your browser.

Built on the experience of 英語漬け.com

英語漬け.com has helped Japanese learners study for many years, with vocabulary quizzes, grammar, reading, AI writing and speaking practice, and tools for schools. Its learning design is based on more than 100 million answered questions. aplus brings the same method to you.

Our mission

Good learning should not depend on where you live or what you can pay. We build free, clear lessons for each country and each language, so anyone can start today.
