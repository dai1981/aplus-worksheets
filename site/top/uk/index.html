FAQ

Are these worksheets free?

Yes. Every lesson, quiz and printable PDF is free. No sign-up or email is needed.

Do they match the National Curriculum in England?

Yes. Years 1–9 follow the National Curriculum, Years 10–11 the GCSE subject content and Years 12–13 the A level subject content.

Do they help with SATs, GCSE and A level?

Yes. Year 6 covers the KS2 SATs skills, Years 10–11 cover GCSE Maths and Combined Science, and Years 12–13 cover A level Maths, Biology, Chemistry and Physics.

What does each topic include?

A short lesson with worked examples and common mistakes, 100 practice questions in 5 sets from easy to hard, instant answers with explanations, and a 4-page printable PDF with an answer key.

Which year should my child start with?

Start with your child's current year. If it feels hard, go back one year for a quick review.

Can teachers use them in class?

Yes. You can print and copy the worksheets for home and classroom use.
