Aplus Japanese Discord

A free place to ask about Japanese words, grammar, anime Japanese and the JLPT.

Community rules

  1. Be kind. Everyone here is learning.
  2. Ask anything about Japanese in #ask-japanese. Please write the word or sentence you mean.
  3. No spam, ads, adult content, hate or personal attacks.
  4. Do not share personal information (address, phone, school, etc.), yours or anyone else's.
  5. Do not ask other members to move to private messages.

Members must be 13 or older (Discord rule).
