Free math practice, made for your child

aplus makes free math worksheets for parents and teachers. Every topic follows the Common Core State Standards, so your child practices exactly what they learn at school.

What you get

  • A short lesson. The key idea, worked examples and the most common mistakes.
  • 100 practice questions. Five sets from easy to hard, with instant answers and explanations.
  • A printable worksheet. Four pages with an answer key, ready for home or the classroom.

Grades and topics

  • Grade 1: 16 topics, including add and subtract within 20, word problems within 20, add three numbers.
  • Grade 2: 16 topics, including addition and subtraction facts, add and subtract within 100, word problems within 100.
  • Grade 3: 16 topics, including multiplication facts, division facts, rounding to 10 and 100.
  • Grade 4: 18 topics, including multi-digit multiplication, long division, equivalent fractions.
  • Grade 5: 17 topics, including add and subtract unlike fractions, multiply fractions, divide fractions.
  • Grade 6: 20 topics, including ratios, unit rates, ratio tables.

Every topic ends with word problems, the kind your child meets in class and on state tests.

Made for you

  • Your curriculum. Each page shows the standard it matches.
  • Your pace. About 10 minutes a day. Questions your child misses come back.
  • Free. No sign-up, no email, no fees.

Who we are

aplus is run by Aplus Inc. in Japan, the team behind 英語漬け.com, one of Japan's largest learning sites. Our lessons are designed from more than 100 million answered questions.
